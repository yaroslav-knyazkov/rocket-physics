\subsubsection{Burnout}
In burnout model the error is derived from after burnout dynamics of form 
\begin{equation}
    m \frac{dv}{dt} = -mg - kv^2.
\end{equation}
By applying approximation
\begin{equation}
    \frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{dv}{dt}\frac{dh}{dh} = \frac{dh}{dt}\frac{dv}{dh} = v \frac{dv}{dh}
\end{equation}
and $u = v^2$. One gets
\begin{equation}
    \frac{du}{dh} = -2g -\frac{2k}{m}u.
\end{equation}
This intern can be integrated
\begin{equation}
    \int_{u}^{u} \frac{du}{-2g -\frac{2k}{m}u} = \int_{h}^{h_f}dh
\end{equation}
where $u = v^2$ at $h$ and $u_f = 0$ and $h_f$, gives sollution of error
\begin{equation}
    e = h_f - h = \frac{m}{2k}\ln\left(1 + \frac{k v^2}{mg}\right).
\end{equation}
